# xX

## Subject Experience

**xX** is the foundational ontology for **Subject Experience**.

> xX is the process through which a subject experiences, interprets, acts upon, and is changed by its interaction with the world, other subjects, environments, tools, information, and work.

The purpose of xX is to provide one common foundation for UX, DX, AX, and RX.

## Canonical cycle

```text
World
  ↓
Observation
  ↓
Experience
  ↓
Cognition
  ↓
Decision
  ↓
Action
  ↓
Outcome
  ↓
Feedback
  ↓
Experience Update
  ↺
```

Experience is not merely an event or a feeling. It is the subject-relative state/process that is formed and updated through interaction.

## Specializations

```text
xX
├── UX = User Experience
├── DX = Developer Experience
├── AX = Agent Experience
└── RX = Research Experience
```

The subject can also be an organization, human, machine, or another actor. UX/DX/AX/RX are specializations of the same xX foundation.

## Core model

```text
Subject × World Interaction
            ↓
           xX
            ↓
   Updated Subject State
            ↓
    Future Interaction
```

## Ontological distinctions

| Concept | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Subject | Entity that can participate in experience |
| World | Context/environment in which interaction occurs |
| Observation | What the subject receives or distinguishes |
| Experience | Subject-relative state/process generated through interaction |
| Cognition | Interpretation, modeling, reasoning, or recognition |
| Decision | Selection among possible responses |
| Action | Intervention by the subject |
| Outcome | Resulting change |
| Feedback | Information from the outcome that can update future experience/action |

## Ecosystem role

xX defines the common experience layer. Specializations and domain repositories define their own concrete problems, workflows, capabilities, and outcomes.

- **UX** — User Experience
- **DX** — Developer Experience
- **AX** — Agent Experience
- **RX** — Research Experience

xX does not replace these repositories; it provides their shared conceptual foundation.
